# v0.18.5 — A Character of Its Own

Every settlement has a **character**, and you choose it at each rung of
the ladder:

- **Market** — its goods are worth 25% more (a higher Prosperity target).
- **Garrison** — it arms its whole population (extra strength for the
  levy).
- **Cathedral** — its Prosperity recovers 1.5× as fast from hardship.

When you raise a village to a Town (or a Town to a City), the panel now
offers the three-way choice — Market Town, Garrison Town, Cathedral Town —
and each is a persistent, modest bonus that reads through the real
economy. Rival realms pick characters too, and starting settlements
(including underground holds) each carry one, so the world's Towns and
Cities read as Market, Garrison, or Cathedral settlements at a glance.
The settlement panel shows a settlement's character and what it does.
