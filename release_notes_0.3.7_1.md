# Shapes of War v0.3.7_1

Right-click during a battle to send your Commander (or any selected troops) straight at a spot or an enemy, MOBA-style — overrides his usual caution about advancing unescorted. Also added a Select All button/hotkey (0) in battle planning.

Fixed: countryside regions with no town of their own could end up with no road connecting them to the rest of your kingdom.
