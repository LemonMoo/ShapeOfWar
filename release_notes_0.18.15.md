# v0.18.15 — No Windows on the Ceiling

The underworld view no longer hands over the overworld for free.

Descending used to paint **the whole landmass silhouette** (the shape of
every continent and coastline), the **surface road and trade network**,
**terrain glyphs** (forests and peaks above you), **ships and caravans**,
**construction sites**, and **realm and region names** — all visible from
inside a cave, as if you had a window on the ceiling.

Now:

- The land silhouette shows **only where you have actually seen the surface
  above** — your own realm and the ground you've explored. A cave under a
  continent you have never seen is still a cave under a continent you have
  never seen.
- Roads, trade lanes, terrain glyphs, ships, surface caravans, construction
  sites, and realm/region names **do not exist below ground**. The under
  layer shows the cave world: rock, galleries, gates, what's actually down
  there — and marches, which are layer-honest already.

Orientation is preserved: where you've been above, the coastline still
reads through the rock; everywhere else is honest, unlit rock until you
earn the view.
