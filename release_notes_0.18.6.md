# v0.18.6 — The Realm Chronicle

A dated history of your realm, written as it happens:

- **The founding** — "Spring 1, Year 1 — The realm of X is founded at Y."
- **The first village** built by your own hands.
- **Every village raised to a Town** — "Grimburrow rises to a Garrison
  Town."
- **Every Town raised to a City** — "Durworth rises to a Cathedral City."
- **Every claim secured.**

Open it from the new **Chronicle** button (next to Compendium). Rival
realms keep their own chronicles too — the seed makes every realm's story
reproducible.

Also in this release:

- **Fixed:** the game is now reproducible from its seed. New games with
  the same seed play out identically — previously the sim drew from
  unseeded randomness, so two same-seed games diverged from day one.
