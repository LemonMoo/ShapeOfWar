# v0.18.2 — The Compendium Pass

A full pass over the in-game encyclopedia (Compendium) after the
settlement-first redesign, so every article describes the rules as they
actually are.

## What changed

- **Settlements & Villages** — rewritten for the new reality: one-City
  starts, camp-gated industry (a village without its Woodcutters'/Mining
  camp extracts nothing), village capacity ("n/m villages"), the Found
  Village / prosperity sources of new villages, and adults recovering as
  children grow up.
- **Founding & Growth (new article)** — the whole ladder in one place:
  Found Village → Raise to Town → Upgrade Town to City, with live costs,
  why it exists (village capacity), and where the actions live.
- **Expansion & Claiming Wildland** — claims are bare land, spoils land
  in your storerooms (not "new villages"), and the development gate
  (your regions must average ≥50% of village capacity) is documented.
- **Prosperity** — City Growth now respects village capacity, and the
  no-forest section covers burning Coal in Winter and the Firewood
  storage exemption.
- **Currency** — claims cost provisions, not Gold (the three stale
  references fixed).
- **Storage & Spoilage** — villages' extractive camps listed, and a new
  "Firewood is never blocked" section.
- **Construction** — the FOUNDING & RAISING ladder section with live
  costs.

Every number is read live from the same constants the game runs on.
