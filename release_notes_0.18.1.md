# v0.18.1 — The Ladder

The settlement-first growth path is now complete: every realm starts as a
single capital and grows by **founding villages, raising them into Towns,
and raising the Towns into Cities**.

## World

- **Every faction starts with exactly one City** and nothing else pre-grown
  — your capital. No starter Towns.
- **Newly claimed regions are bare**: the automatic "homestead" village is
  gone. Expansion now means building the land up yourself, one deliberate
  step at a time.

## Growth — the ladder

- **Found a Village** (new): cheap and fast (8 turns), consumes one of the
  region's village slots. The only way to put people on bare claimed land.
  Region panel → Found Village..., with the same placement hints as
  settlements.
- **Raise to Town** (new): a village grows into a Town in place (12 turns
  — stone and provisions, no timber) — it keeps its people, stores and
  name, and the Town adds its village-slot allowance (+4).
- **Upgrade Town to City** (existing): the final rung — the biggest jump in
  village capacity, tax and population.
- The AI climbs the same ladder.

## Balance

- The one-City start makes the opening slower and more deliberate: your
  capital's 11 village slots fill gradually, then each raise or upgrade is
  the next step outward.
