# v0.19.0

Dry Stone: a mountain realm's stone is its timber.

## Timberless land builds in stone

- **A region with no forest anywhere — no forest, taiga or jungle cells in
  the whole region (a mountain or highland homeland, tundra, desert, or the
  underground) — pays every building's Logs line as Stone instead**, at 2
  Stone per Log. Storage, mining and woodcutters' camps, gold mines,
  towns, castles, cities: all of them go up in dressed stone there.
- **This is what fixes the mountain deadlock.** Logs only grow on
  forest/taiga/jungle at low elevation, so a mountain realm could never
  produce them — yet its first storage, its camps (which unlock its ore),
  and its towns were all priced in Logs. A mountain realm would stall its
  whole early game on a forest it cannot grow, while sitting on all the
  stone a builder could want. Now it builds in stone, the same way it
  already burns coal for heat in Winter.
- **The rule is per-region, not per-realm**, and it is shown out loud: the
  build menu notes "Dry stone — this region has no timber, so Logs are
  paid as Stone" wherever it applies. Forest realms never see it anywhere.
- **AI realms play by the same rule**, so a mountain AI faction's camps and
  towns come up in stone too.

## Ships are the exception

- **A hull is genuinely wood.** Building a ship still costs real Logs, and
  a timberless realm must trade for them — the one cost the stone rule
  never touches. (The shipyard itself is masonry and does substitute.)
