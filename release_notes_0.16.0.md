# v0.16.0 — Where You Make Your Stand

You used to be dropped into your people's best homeland and find out afterwards
what you'd been given. Now you choose, and you see the ground before you take
it.

## Choose your start

The New Game map marks good starting sites in gold. Click one to found your
realm there — or click anywhere else you like the look of. The map you are
choosing on is the map you will play: picking a spot regrows the same world
with you founded on it, terrain unchanged.

## See it first

Click a site and a card tells you what the land actually is: its country
(plains, forest, hills and the rest), the goods its ground can raise, how much
of it is farmland, whether it has a coast or a river within reach, how crowded
it is, and how well it suits the people you have chosen.

## Warned, not forbidden

Pick barren rock, sand or ice and the game tells you plainly that a realm
founded there cannot feed itself — and then lets you do it anyway, if that is
the game you want. Thin farmland gets a gentler note about leaning on trade.

Press Play without choosing and nothing changes from before: you are placed in
your people's best available homeland.

Existing saves are untouched.
