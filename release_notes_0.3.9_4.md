## v0.3.9_4 - Fantasy HUD Redesign, Road Merging, Zoom-Based Village View

- NEW: a fantasy-themed HUD facelift across the whole game -- warmer parchment colors, a gold accent, bigger readable text, taller buttons, and foldable SUMMARY cards on the faction/region panels instead of one long paragraph of stats.
- NEW: the resource bar, alerts, treasury, trade log, and battle screen all match the same parchment/gold styling now, instead of the old dark-blue chrome. The Treasury popup is foldable sections instead of one long printout.
- FIXED: newly built roads and dirt paths cut their own line right beside an existing road instead of joining it -- they now prefer sharing an existing road's route and only split off where they actually diverge.
- NEW: villages now appear and become clickable simply by zooming in close on a region -- no more double-clicking a region and hoping it registers.
