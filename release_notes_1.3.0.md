# v1.3.0

The crown takes its cut — and spends it on the realm.

## The kingdom treasury

Every realm now keeps a **kingdom treasury**: a central pot, separate from the
coin sitting in its settlements and villages. Two taxes fill it.

- **Income tax** — every settlement pays up to its civic-wealth rate each day,
  drawn from the gold it actually holds. A settlement holding no gold pays
  nothing.
- **Transaction tax** — 10% of the gold that changes hands in a sale is
  skimmed off the seller's receipt into the crown's pot.

Taxes **redistribute, never mint**: they only move coin that already exists.
Gold still enters the world solely through mining Gold Ore and striking it at a
Mint — taxation never conjures it.

## The treasury funds development

The Gold line of development is now paid from the treasury, not the
settlements' own stock:

- buildings, storage works, shipyards and tunnels,
- founding towns, castles and cities (and raising a village to a Town or a
  Town to a City),
- and founding a village now costs coin too.

Claims remain the exception — they cost settlers and provisions, not gold.

## You can see it

The **Treasury** panel (click the Gold row in the resources bar) gains a
**KINGDOM TREASURY** section: the balance, and its own ledger of income tax,
trade tax and development spend over recent turns.
