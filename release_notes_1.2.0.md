# v1.2.0

Every realm rules its own way — and its people's loyalty decides how well it runs.

## Government by consent

- **Every realm has a government form.** Elder Council, Clan Council, Guild
  Oligarchy, Theocracy, Feudal Monarchy, Republic, Warband, and Gang-Boss.
  Each species prefers some over others — Elves sit in Elder Councils, Dwarves
  in Clan Councils, Orcs in Warbands, Goblins under a Boss — and a realm is
  born under the form its people score highest.
- **Loyalty is a live thing.** A realm's loyalty toward its form starts at its
  species' preference and then moves, easing each day toward a target that
  rewards behaving as that form's people want: raid for your warband, trade
  for your republic, consecrate for your theocracy — and don't sprawl an elder
  council into war.
- **Reform is a choice with a price.** Your realm panel lists every form with
  its affinity and the loyalty it costs; reforming against the grain is dear,
  reforming with it is cheap.

## Loyalty runs the realm

- **Economy** — a loyal realm's settlements are wealthier, and the species'
  economy stat (long declared, never used) finally matters.
- **Army** — a loyal realm musters more of its levy; a disloyal one's army
  melts.
- **Land** — loyalty adds or removes regions of governance capacity, so an
  unpopular government's rule thins at the edges.
- **Revolt** — let loyalty sink far enough and frontier regions rise and break
  away.

## A modal that stays in the game

- Frontier events no longer open a separate OS window that could open
  unfocused or fall behind the map. They now draw over a full-window blocker
  on the game's own window, and the shortcuts stay inert until you choose.
