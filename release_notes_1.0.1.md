# v1.0.1

Dressed Stone: the dry-stone rule follows the tier ladder.

## Dry-stone goes deep

- **In a timberless region — no forest, taiga or jungle anywhere (a
  mountain or highland homeland, tundra, desert, or the underground) — the
  tier-2+ timber good Planks is now paid as Stone too**, at 3 Stone per
  Plank. That is exactly Planks' value on the resource ladder (Planks are a
  tier-4 good at 9 gold, Stone tier-2 at 3 gold), so a tier-2 building that
  in a forest realm is timber-framed with sawn planks becomes dressed
  stonework in a timberless one — paid for in the stone that region
  actually digs.
- **Tier-1 Logs keep the v0.19.0 rate** (2 Stone per Log). The rule now
  covers the whole ladder: tier-2 storage, camps, gold mines, holding
  halls and guild halls no longer stall on a sawmill a mountain realm
  cannot run.
- **The stone rule substitutes timber, not clay.** Bricks and every
  non-timber cost line stay exactly as listed — a timberless realm makes
  no clay, so its advanced masonry is genuinely brick (imported or won),
  while its timber bill is paid in its own stone.
- **AI realms play by the same rule**, and the build menu notes "Dry stone
  — this region has no timber, so Logs and Planks are paid as Stone"
  wherever it applies.

## Ships are still the exception

- **A hull is genuinely wood.** Shipbuilding keeps its real Logs cost and
  is never substituted — the one timber bill a timberless realm must trade
  for.
