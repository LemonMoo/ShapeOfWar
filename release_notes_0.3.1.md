# Shapes of War v0.3.1

Before you played a single turn, New Game asked two questions and told you
almost nothing. Now it's where you actually build your realm — and where you
see the world you're about to rule before you commit to it.

---

## A realm worth naming

**Name your ruler.** Every realm has a monarch now, not just yours — a rival
realm has someone sitting its throne, with a title drawn from their people:
King or Queen, Archon, High King, Warlord, Boss. When you go to war with a
neighbour, you're fighting someone, not a flag.

**Pick your banner colour** from a palette built around your own species —
enough choice to make the realm feel like yours, without breaking the one rule
that makes the political map readable: kin look like kin, and no rival sits on
top of your own colour. Pick anything on offer and the world adjusts around
you.

**Choose the shape of the world.** Small (tight, 9 realms), Standard (14, the
old default), or Large (room to grow, 18) — plus a slider for exactly how many
rivals you want in it.

## See it before you play it

The New Game screen now shows you the actual map, with your starting position
marked, before you press Play. Charting a big world takes a little while, so it
happens in the background from the moment you open the screen — keep naming
things, picking colours, browsing species, and the preview is usually ready
by the time you're done deciding who you want to be.

Species are laid out side by side with their real stat differences and
signature units, instead of one at a time — pick with the numbers in front of
you, not a guess.

Not happy with the roll? Reroll the world without losing anything you've
already named.
