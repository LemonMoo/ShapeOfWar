# v0.18.7 — The Frontier

Newly claimed land is now a **frontier**: for its first weeks under your
banner it can throw small events at you —

- **Bandits** demanding tribute (pay, or drive them off — they may burn a
  homestead).
- **Scouts** finding rich soil (a gift of Food).
- **Old ruins** holding salvage (Logs, Stone, Gold).
- **A hermit's blessing** (a lift to the new land's Prosperity).
- **Wanderers** asking to settle (take them in, or send them on with
  provisions).

The world pauses while you decide; rival realms settle their own
frontiers unseen. Every event is a small, one-time choice or discovery —
texture for the slow expansion, not a swing.
