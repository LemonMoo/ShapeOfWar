# v1.1.0

The economy ledger balances — and now it's proven.

## A ledger that balances

- **Every number is checked against the world.** The ledger's day-by-day and
  this-year breakdowns are attributed at phase boundaries and reconciled to
  each realm's real stock — and the reconciliation check measures a run's own
  window now. A save loaded mid-year carries its year-to-date history, which
  the old check counted twice; the ledger is never wrong about what a realm
  holds.
- **The whole test suite's economy proofs are green.** Recording the ledger
  still changes nothing in the simulated world (the A/B observation proof
  passes), the new year starts its totals fresh (no year-leak), and the
  ledger panel renders with data and with an empty ledger — all on the
  regenerated dev worlds.
