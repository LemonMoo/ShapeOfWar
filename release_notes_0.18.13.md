# v0.18.13 — The Door

The underground realm's **supply line** is here: a cave realm's hold and
its gate town now trade **through the door**.

- **Down:** when the door-side has surplus food and the hold is hungry, a
  wagon carries it down the two-layer route into the galleries. This is
  what makes underground growth possible — you grow as much as you can
  feed through the door.
- **Up:** the hold's cave goods — ore, gems, crafts — stage at the gate
  town's market, its export door. Never its own food: a hold's grain is
  its lifeblood, and the lifeline deliberately does not drain a marginal
  hold.
- **The blockade:** a foreign army standing at the door's surface mouth
  *holds the gate* — no supplies move that turn. Taking a cave realm now
  has two honest strategies: **starve it** (camp the door) or **storm it**
  (the two-layer march through a gate, which already works).
