# v0.18.17 — The Front Gate

A cave realm's capital now has **its own door**.

The great hall sat in the deepest rock — defensible — but the network's
openings were all at its far edge, so the capital was a day's march from
the nearest way up, and the gate town could sit at any random door.
Now:

- A **private shaft is cut straight up from the great hall** to the
  surface: the capital's *Front Gate*. You can walk out of your own city.
- The **gate town is the realm's front gate** — it always sits on the
  capital's door, directly above the hall.
- The **mountainside over the shaft** is the hold's own front terraces —
  the great hall's fields are right at its door.
- The **trade lifeline runs through the front door** — and it is now
  stable: blockading one door can't be quietly sidestepped by rerouting
  the supplies through another.

Also fixed: a crash when the AI upgraded a town in a region that had never
run the village pass (mid-game wildland claims) — regions now always carry
their village and settlement lists.
