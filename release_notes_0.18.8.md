# v0.18.8 — The Seat of the Realm

Your founding City is the capital — **the Seat of the Realm** — and people
flock to a capital. It grows toward its population ceiling at the frontier
rate (the fast growth Villages and Towns enjoy), far faster than any other
City: feed and shelter it and your one starting city becomes a true
metropolis within a few years, while every other city you build must earn
its people the slow way.

The capital's settlement panel and the Compendium both say so.
