# v0.18.19 — The Mountain Doors

The New Game screen now shows a cave people where the doors are, gives the
map room to breathe, and stops leaving a ghost behind when you move your
start.

- **Mountain doors on the map.** With a Dwarf or Goblin selected, the world
  preview marks every underground entrance — each cave network's mountain
  door, the surface mouth of its adits — with a **violet diamond**. You can
  pick your start by one instead of hoping the gold dots land near a way in,
  and the chosen site's card already tells you whether it sits above a cave
  network.
- **A much larger preview.** The map box grows from 430×258 to 640×384
  (about 2.2× the area) and the window with it (1180×720 → 1360×800, still
  resizable down to 1024×640), so the landmasses and the doors are actually
  readable before you commit.
- **No stray circle.** The white ring that marked the *default* start used
  to stay behind after you clicked a different spot, so the preview showed
  two starts at once. Once you choose, the default ring disappears and only
  your chosen start's green ring remains.
