# Shapes of War v0.3.7

World generation is tectonic now: mountain ranges rise where plates collide, rifts open where they pull apart, coastal trenches form at subduction zones, and island chains trail away from volcanic hotspots. Land is still exactly 40% of the map, every time.

This is a first pass, not a finished tuning — some worlds come out more island-fragmented than intended, and river/lake density has shifted slightly and needs re-tuning. Both are being worked on.
