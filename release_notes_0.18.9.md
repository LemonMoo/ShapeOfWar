# v0.18.9 — Season News

At every season change, the world speaks: a one-line digest of what the
realms actually did last season, drawn from their real histories —

- *"Winter 1, Year 3 — Two realms raised new Towns; the Goblin realm
  claimed new land."*
- *"Summer 1, Year 1 — 12 realms founded their first villages."*

Quiet seasons stay quiet — the banner only speaks when there's something
worth saying. Your rivals' progress is visible now, not happening behind
the fog.
