# v0.14.1 — A Legible Hand

Two fixes for readability regressions in the v0.14.0 interface redesign.

- **The selected people on the New Game screen could not be read.** The picked
  row was filled bright gold with its name also in gold — gold on gold. It is
  now marked the way a good list marks a selection: a gold bar down the left
  edge and the name in gold, on a row that stays dark so every word of its
  stats is legible.
- **The resource groups would not open.** Clicking Food, Industry or any other
  group in the left-hand resources panel did nothing. They fold and unfold
  again now.

Nothing else changed. Saves load and play exactly as before.
