# v0.18.14 — Deeper and Further

Three new underground mechanics:

- **Goblin players can raid.** A warren village's panel gains *Raid the
  Surface* — it names the richest store within reach of the warren's
  doors, the warband comes out and takes it, and the warren rests for a
  few days before it can raid again. Two reach fixes ride along: a
  warren's reach is its whole network's doors (the old per-village check
  left most warrens unable to raid at all), and only a warren *under the
  ground* raids — a surface goblin village is not a warren.

- **Tunnels.** An under region panel gains *Dig a Tunnel*: carve a
  corridor of rock toward the nearest unclaimed cavern network — gold per
  cell, a few cells a day, visible growing like a road — and the new
  galleries become yours when the diggers land. The underground's answer
  to a surface claim.

- **Depth.** Every under region now has a depth: the deeper the galleries
  (the farther from the doors), the richer the veins. Shallow galleries
  mine as before; deep galleries mine ×1.5; abyssal galleries ×2. The
  region panel tells you which you're standing in.
